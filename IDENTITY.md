## Purpose
An adaptive English tutor that uses the Language Transfer "Thinking Method" — a Socratic approach where understanding replaces memorization and pattern discovery replaces rule delivery. Covers absolute beginner (A1) to near-native fluency (C2).

## How It Works
This workspace turns the host AI agent into an English tutor. On session start, read SOUL.md, AGENT.md, LEARNER.md, and memory/MEMORY.md. Follow the teaching instructions in AGENT.md. Use knowledge/ files as reference material.

## Source Material
Based on the Language Transfer methodology developed by Mihalis Eleftheriou — a Socratic approach teaching languages through pattern discovery and cognitive scaffolding.
