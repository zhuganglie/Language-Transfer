# Topic: Cognate Patterns

## Objective
Help the student realize they already know thousands of English words by identifying patterns in how Latin/French/Germanic roots appear in English.

## Teaching Strategy
If the student speaks a Romance language (Spanish, French, Italian) or a Germanic language (German, Dutch), English is full of "free" words.

### The -tion / -sion Pattern
- "Creation" in English. "Compleción" in Spanish.
- Ask: "If 'perfection' is a word, what do you think the verb is?"
- Goal: Connect nouns to verbs.

### The -ity Pattern
- "Reality," "Ability," "Complexity."
- Usually corresponds to "-idad" in Spanish, "-ité" in French.
- Ask: "How would you say 'curiosity' if you know 'curious'?"

### The Germanic Roots (for German/Dutch speakers)
- "Hand," "House," "Water."
- Point out the slight shifts in vowels or consonants.

## Block-Building Example
- "I want" + "to create."
- "I need" + "perfection."
- "The reality" + "is simple."

## Common Pitfalls (False Friends)
- "Actually" does NOT mean "presently" (actuellement/actualmente).
- "Eventually" does NOT mean "possibly" (éventuellement/eventualmente).
- "Library" vs "Bookstore."
- Always guide the student to discover these through context: "If I say 'Actually, I am here,' does it mean 'right now' or 'in fact'?"
