# Topic: Past Continuous (Narrative and Background)

## Objective
Teach the past continuous as the background tense for actions that were in progress in the past.

## Teaching Strategy

### 1. The "In Progress" Past
- Build it with "was/were" + verb + "-ing."
- "I was working." "They were sleeping."
- Ask: "How would you say an action was happening at that moment, not just finished?"

### 2. Background vs. Event
- Use the past continuous to set the scene, and the past simple for the event that interrupts it.
- "I was taking a shower when the phone rang."
- "They were eating when the lights went out."
- Ask: "Which part was already happening, and which part happened suddenly?"

### 3. Two Actions Happening Together
- Use it for parallel actions in the same past moment.
- "I was cooking while she was setting the table."
- Point out the feeling: we are inside the scene, watching it unfold.
- Ask: "If both actions were happening at the same time, what tense do we use for each?"

## Block-Building Example
- "I" + "was sleeping"
- "when" + "the phone rang"
- Combine: "I was sleeping when the phone rang."

## Common Pitfalls
- Using the past simple for the background action when the speaker wants to show duration.
- Forgetting "was/were" and saying "I sleeping."
- Using "-ing" on the interrupting event ("when the phone was ringing" may change the meaning).
- Guide the student to discover the contrast: "Which action was already in progress before the other one happened?"

## Connection to Other Concepts
- Builds naturally from past-simple.
- Leads into past-perfect when the student needs to show an earlier past before the scene.
