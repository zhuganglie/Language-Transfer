# Topic: Present Continuous (Happening Now)

## Objective
Teach the form and meaning of the present continuous for actions in progress and temporary situations.

## Teaching Strategy

### 1. Build It with "Be" + "-ing"
- "I am working," "she is reading," "they are waiting."
- The helper carries the person: am, is, are.
- The main verb shows the action in progress with "-ing."
- Ask: "If the action is happening right now, what helper do we need before the verb?"

### 2. Contrast It with Present Simple
- Present simple is for habits: "I work every day."
- Present continuous is for now: "I am working right now."
- Always teach them as a contrast, not as separate islands.
- Ask: "Which one sounds like a routine, and which one sounds like this exact moment?"

### 3. Use It for Temporary Situations
- Not only "now this second," but also temporary periods: "I am living with a friend this month."
- That helps the student feel why English chooses this form.
- Ask: "If the situation is temporary, does English often sound more natural with the continuous?"

## Block-Building Example
- "I" + "am" + "studying."
- "He" + "is" + "working" + "from home."
- "They" + "are" + "waiting" + "for the bus."

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting the helper: "I working."
- Using present simple for actions happening now.
- Overusing the continuous with state verbs: "I am knowing" instead of "I know."
- Guide the student to discover: "Is this an action unfolding, or a fact that simply exists?"
