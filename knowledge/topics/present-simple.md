# Topic: Present Simple (Habits and Truths)

## Objective
Teach the use of the present simple for general truths, routines, and habits, and introduce the third-person singular "-s."

## Teaching Strategy

### 1. General Truths and Habits
- "I work every day."
- "The sun rises in the east."
- Ask: "What is something you do every single morning?"

### 2. The Third-Person Singular "-s"
- This is the most common error.
- "I work" but "He works."
- "I like" but "She likes."
- Ask: "If you like coffee, and your friend likes coffee too, how do you say that for your friend?"

### 3. Question and Negative Formation with "Do/Does"
- Questions: "Do you like...?" / "Does he like...?"
- Negatives: "I do not like..." / "She does not like..."
- Point out that once "does" takes the "-s," the main verb loses it: "She does like" (Not "She does likes").
- Ask: "How would you ask me if I speak another language?"

## Block-Building Example
- "He" + "works" + "at a hospital."
- "Does" + "she" + "want" + "a coffee?"
- "I" + "do not" + "know" + "the answer."

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting the "-s" for he/she/it.
- Using "do" instead of "does" for the third person.
- Keeping the "-s" in questions/negatives (e.g., "Does he works?").
- Guide the student to discover these: "If 'does' is already doing the 'special' work for 'he', do we need another 's' on 'work'?"
